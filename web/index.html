Collect · verify · audit

Count X votes with evidence, not guesswork.

x-vote observes native polls, replies, likes, reposts and other public activity through open-source adapters. It keeps every countable event traceable, separates anonymous aggregates from individually evidenced activity, and never invents missing data.

  • No official X API, no hidden automation.
  • Authorized sessions only; rate limits and access controls are respected.
  • Temporary sessions are cleaned up on exit; exports are explicit and local.

What you get
